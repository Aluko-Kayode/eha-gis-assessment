# MEMO: Bansara State SIA, March 2026, Coverage Gap Assessment

**To:** Emergency Response Manager  
**From:** GIS/Data Analytics  
**Re:** Where to send a follow up team, and a data quality flag

**Bottom line:** 253 of 2,562 planned settlements, about 10%, show no confirmed 
visit from either GPS tracks or paper e-tally records. These are not scattered 
randomly across the state, they concentrate disproportionately in Katsuma LGA, 
and follow up resources should go there first.

## Findings

1. **Coverage gap.** 253 accessible, planned settlements have no GPS confirmed 
visit and no e-tally record. This excludes the 75 settlements pre classified as 
inaccessible before the round began, those were a known, deliberate exclusion, 
not a gap.

2. **Katsuma is the priority, not Idi-Oro.** By raw count, Idi-Oro has the most 
missed settlements (109), but it also has by far the most settlements overall. 
As a share of settlements planned, Katsuma is worst hit at 14.9% missed, nearly 
three times Ilela's rate (5.3%). Recommend Katsuma as the first follow up priority.

3. **No tight geographic clustering.** A statistical test found the missed 
settlements are not concentrated in specific hotspots within LGAs, they're 
dispersed roughly the way any random subset would be. This means the gap is not 
one blocked road or one bad neighborhood, it looks more like a broad, LGA level 
shortfall, consistent with under resourcing or route planning gaps at the LGA 
level rather than a single localized obstacle.

4. **Paper reporting undercounts real coverage.** GPS confirms more settlement 
visits (2,225) than the e-tally records (2,023). Teams are visiting settlements 
they are not fully capturing on paper. This suggests the true coverage picture 
is somewhat better than e-tally alone would suggest, but also that e-tally 
cannot be trusted alone for coverage decisions going forward.

5. **Data quality flag.** 42 settlements are recorded in the e-tally with doses 
administered, but GPS never confirms a team was physically present. This 
warrants follow up before counting them as covered, it may be a settlement ID 
transcription error, a reporting error, or in rare cases a fabricated entry.

**Recommendation:** Deploy the follow up team to Katsuma first, using the 
attached settlement list and map. Independently verify the 42 e-tally-only 
settlements before counting them as covered.

---
*Data quality note: a small number of GPS loggers, one in particular, produced 
physically impossible readings (night time movement at 300 to 480 km/h, 
coordinates at 0,0). This is a hardware fault, not team conduct, and those 
loggers should be pulled and replaced before the next round.*
